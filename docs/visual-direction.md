# Visual Direction

## Product feel

Calm, dark-first operations dashboard for a fitness studio.
Clean, structured, practical, and modern.
Not a marketing website.
Not playful.
Not flashy.
Not “sporty”.

## Primary UX goal

Help users scan information fast and perform actions with low friction.

## Visual tone

- professional
- clear
- modern
- restrained
- slightly premium

## Layout feel

- dashboard-first
- left sidebar
- top header
- content in cards/sections
- predictable page structure
- good scanning rhythm

## Density

Balanced-to-compact.
Should support tables, forms, statuses, and quick actions.

## Theme

Dark theme by default.
Neutral dark surfaces with clear layering and readable contrast.

## Styling direction

- neutral dark surfaces
- visible borders and dividers
- soft radius
- limited shadows
- one accent color
- clear but restrained status colors

## Components feel

- inputs simple and readable
- buttons functional first
- cards clean and structured
- section headers obvious
- empty states minimal

## Avoid

- landing-page aesthetics
- glassmorphism
- heavy gradients
- excessive animation
- decorative clutter
- fitness-app gimmicks
